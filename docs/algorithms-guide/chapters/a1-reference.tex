\section{Checked-in platform profiles}
\label{sec:platformprofiles}

These are the effective values in the current source with 64-bit limbs
and no command-line threshold overrides. All entries are limb counts.
The product floor counts the \emph{sum} of operand lengths. A product
must also pass the short-operand and area tests in \cref{ch:muldispatch}.
The square floors apply to the square dispatcher. The BZ floor controls
when division starts considering Burnikel--Ziegler.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
Profile & NTT product & NTT square & Parked square & BZ divisor \\
\midrule
Generic & 640 & 352 & 608 & 96 \\
Linux x86-64 GCC & 8\,192 & 4\,096 & 4\,096 & 81 \\
Linux x86-64 Clang & 8\,192 & 4\,096 & 4\,096 & 96 \\
macOS Arm64 Clang & 640 & 352 & 512 & 81 \\
macOS x86-64 Clang & 4\,096 & 768 & 4\,096 & 96 \\
Windows x86-64 Clang & 4\,096 & 2\,048 & 4\,096 & 96 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The macOS Arm64 Clang profile also sets the single-thread floor for
distinct, nearly equal operands to 704 limbs. Source builds normally
select the profile for the compiler target. Packaged builds use the
profile embedded at their tagged revision. See \file{docs/PLATFORM\_TUNING.md}.
All checked-in profiles use the generic add and subtract cutoffs.

\section{Further reading}

\begin{itemize}
  \item D.~E. Knuth, \emph{The Art of Computer Programming}, Vol.~2, §4.3: classical multiple-precision
        arithmetic and Algorithm D.
  \item R.~P. Brent and P.~Zimmermann, \emph{Modern Computer Arithmetic}, Cambridge University Press,
        2010: Karatsuba, Toom-Cook, FFT multiplication, Newton division, base conversion.
  \item C.~Burnikel and J.~Ziegler, ``Fast Recursive Division'', MPI-I-98-1-022, 1998.
  \item N.~Möller and T.~Granlund, ``Improved Division by Invariant Integers'', \emph{IEEE Transactions on
        Computers} 60(2), 2011.
  \item M.~Bodrato, ``Towards Optimal Toom-Cook Multiplication for Univariate and Multivariate
        Polynomials in Characteristic 2 and 0'', WAIFI 2007.
  \item V.~Shoup, NTL library: precomputed-quotient modular multiplication.
  \item D.~H. Bailey, ``FFTs in External or Hierarchical Memory'', \emph{J. Supercomputing} 4, 1990:
        the six-step FFT.
  \item The library's own technical notes, with full measurement history:
        \file{docs/MULTIPLICATION.md}, \file{docs/DIVISION.md}, \file{docs/STRING\_CONVERSION.md},
        \file{docs/BASE.md}, \file{docs/THREAD\_SAFETY.md}, \file{BENCHMARK.md}.
